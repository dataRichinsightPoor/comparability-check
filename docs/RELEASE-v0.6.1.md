This release publishes the revised companion essay, “To Be or Not to Be Comparable,” and connects it to five earlier Data-Rich, Insight-Poor essays through scientific interpretation grounded in mechanistic and measurement understanding.

- Continuous scientific narrative integrating cell expression, depletion, avidity, binding, competition, functional response, crossed comparisons, QC, and GMP.
- Three figures: the original historical-equivalence simulation and two attributed literature-panel crops.

- Five related essays integrated into the argument rather than a separate reading list.

## Verification and scope

All 196 local tests pass. Reproduction of 1.8 million summary-endpoint experiments matches the checked-in scenario results; the masked historical-equivalence counts are 2,869, 7,830, and 40,589 per 100,000 experiments at 4, 8, and 16 independent contrasts. These are synthetic operating characteristics, not empirical QC failure rates.
