What did not become established?

An antibody defect, weaker intrinsic affinity, failed integration, equivalence across cells, or functional inhibition by rituximab.

What evidence would separate the explanations?
  • Cell state: track positive fraction and the distribution of accessible sites per positive cell across passage, alongside viability, cell count, culture history, and the gating definition.
  • Integration versus expression: distinguish DNA copy/integrity and integration-site evidence from transcript abundance and surface protein. A drop in fluorescence alone does not identify which layer changed.
  • Antibody versus cell effect: test the same aliquot and a stable reference in the cell contexts being compared. Keep cell passage and assay endpoint explicit rather than pooling unlike measurements.
  • Binding versus blockade: identify what is blocked, its concentration, and the measured outcome. A downstream assay additionally needs a justified relation between occupancy and response.

INSPECT THE ASSUMPTIONS

Comparable for which conclusion?

This is a bounded comparison model, not a dose-response fitter or a regulatory validation system. The historical and bridge views analyze supplied EC50 values; the cell-context view generates synthetic curves to expose different explanations, not fit or diagnose a real assay.

Historical contrast

The teaching model is E(lot, method) = θ(lot) × m(method). Two diagonal observations determine only the product of the lot ratio and method ratio. Slider values are sensitivity scenarios; no distribution or plausible bound for the method effect is inferred.

Method-by-lot interactions, sample aging, and era effects can make this simple separation inadequate. More precise diagonal measurements cannot fill the missing comparisons.

Crossed bridge

For block i, dP = ln(BP) − ln(AP), dQ = ln(BQ) − ln(AQ), and dI = dQ − dP. Each estimate is exp(mean(d)); its interval is exp(mean(d) ± t × SD(d)/√n).

The model requires independent blocks and approximately normal block-level log contrasts. It does not model unequal fit precision, outliers, raw-curve error, missing cells, or unpaired designs. Small n can give unreliable intervals if those assumptions fail.

Equivalence is endpoint-specific

A 90% interval strictly inside justified, prespecified margins corresponds to a two-one-sided equivalence test at α = 0.05 (Lakens, 2017). The 95% intervals are provided separately in the table; a nonsignificant difference is not an equivalence result.

Decisions are per contrast, not a multiplicity-adjusted family of independent claims. Zero observed contrast variation or only one block withholds uncertainty and equivalence. The tool never declares whole-product biosimilarity.

Why EC50 is not automatically affinity

Live-cell endpoint estimates depend on equilibrium and receptor-concentration conditions (Spiegelberg et al., 2021). Rituximab binding on Daudi cells also shows bivalent stabilization and concentration-dependent dynamics (Bondza et al., 2020).

Those observations motivate caution; they do not supply a correction factor for your assay. A retained old vial represents its current state, not automatically its state at original testing.

References help under tested conditions

A common rituximab reference improved cross-laboratory agreement, with limited cell-binding data and checks on dose-response similarity (Prior et al., 2018). That finding does not authorize arbitrary normalization across unbridged methods.

Reference normalization is not implemented here. Do not substitute reference-normalized potency values for EC50 without redefining the endpoint and its direction.

Expression is not integration

Integrated transgenes can be silenced, and expression loss can appear as fewer positive cells or lower expression within cells (Cabrera et al., 2022). The model prescribes those two trajectories separately; it does not derive them from genomic integration quality or distinguish silencing from cell selection.

Raji stability is a counterfactual control here. Actual Raji-derived populations can change CD20 expression under selection (CD20 regulation study).

Population signal versus normalized binding

Positive fraction p and sites per positive cell ρ determine the all-cell arithmetic-mean plateau, proportional to pρ. Positive-only gating instead reports a plateau proportional to ρ, while the full sample still determines ligand consumption. Neither output models median fluorescence or a threshold-defined positive gate.

With a homogeneous one-site equilibrium and negligible depletion, normalization cancels amplitude and EC50 = K. With finite ligand supply, EC50 in total added ligand = K + RT/2 (Hulme & Trevethick, 2010). Lower expression can therefore reduce signal while shifting EC50 left, with K unchanged.

Competition is not downstream function

Fluorescent rituximab displacement on Raji cells has been used as a CD20 competition-binding assay, separately from downstream activities (Visser et al., 2013). Here, an ideal homologous tracer has the same one-site K as the competitor, and no label-induced change in binding.

Without depletion, competitive IC50 = K + tracer concentration. With depletion, the model solves both ligand and receptor conservation rather than applying a free-ligand approximation to total concentrations (Hulme & Trevethick, 2010).

The generic functional layer is a separate thought experiment

The optional reporter maps occupied tracer sites per positive cell x to F(x) = xʰ/(S50ʰ + xʰ). Its normalized inhibition is 1 − F(x(C))/F(x(0)). With h = 1 and no depletion, its IC50 = (K + tracer) × (1 + x(0)/S50), derived from that assumed transfer function.

This shows how response reserve can separate a functional midpoint from an occupancy midpoint. It is not a CD20 signaling mechanism, a prediction of rituximab function, or an inference from cell binding alone. Full-length antibody avidity, receptor organization, trafficking, nonequilibrium kinetics, and heterogeneous positive-cell densities are outside this release.
