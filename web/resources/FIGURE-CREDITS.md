## Original simulation figure

`misleading-historical-equivalence.png` and its SVG, caption, and data files are original model outputs. The figure is pinned to v0.5.0 synthetic results; the v0.6.0 model retains the same algorithm. See https://github.com/dataRichinsightPoor/comparability-check/tree/v0.5.0.

## Literature panels

Chang-Han Lee et al. (2019), “An engineered human Fc domain that behaves like a pH-toggle switch for ultra-long circulation persistence,” Nature Communications 10, 5031. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-13108-2. Full text and license: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6834678/.

© The Authors 2019. CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. No separate third-party credit restriction was identified on the reproduced panels.

`lee-2019-fcrn-density.png` crops panels 1b–c. `lee-2019-raji-function.png` crops panels 3b–c. Cropping only: original axes, data, legends, and panel labels are retained. Original downloaded PNGs are preserved alongside these crops. The article's correction concerns panel 1d, which is not reproduced: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6877538/.

Figure 1 original PNG: https://media.springernature.com/lw1200/springer-static/image/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-019-13108-2/MediaObjects/41467_2019_13108_Fig1_HTML.png.

Figure 3 original PNG: https://media.springernature.com/full/springer-static/image/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-019-13108-2/MediaObjects/41467_2019_13108_Fig3_HTML.png.

The 585-pixel-wide density crop is a web-resolution excerpt, not a newly generated high-resolution dataset. Upscaling would not add scientific detail. For print at larger dimensions obtain the publisher's native-resolution artwork.

The figures of Prior et al. (2018) and Li et al. (2025) were not reproduced: their CC BY-NC-ND licensing differs from the permissive CC BY panels used here. Their findings remain cited in the text.
